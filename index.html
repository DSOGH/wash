3. Workshop Execution Protocol (Wheel-Well Method)
RIGGING SAFETY NOTICE: Engine support bar must be anchored over double-folded towels on fender channels. Lock front bar feet with C-clamps to prevent forward movement on sloped G20 chassis tracks.
  1. Topside Isolation: Remove engine plastics, position support bridge, lock feet with clamps, and tension chains to lifting eyes.
  2. Side Access: Jack front end, secure stands, remove front wheels and plastic inner fender liners.
  3. Main Release: Remove two lower center bolts (07-14-8-869-310) from underneath the subframe.
  4. Side Extraction: Use long extension with swivel joint to unbolt aluminum engine brackets from the block (E12/E14) and pull out as single assemblies.
  5. Bench Swap & Torque: Swap rubber mounts on workbench, reinstall brackets to block (Torque: 28 Nm), set engine weight down naturally, and torque bottom bolts (Torque: 56 Nm).
